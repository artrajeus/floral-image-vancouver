## BYO verification (checked 9 Sep 2026, deniutemuster.com.au FAQs)

- Camping area: **one carton per adult for the entire festival, max 30 cans** — of beer **or pre-mix spirits**, or a 4L cask of wine.
- **No glass** anywhere.
- **No BYO into the Festival Arena** — alcohol taken to the arena gate is surrendered and not returned.
- Random police checks throughout; buying and ferrying extra alcohol in by bus/taxi/car is prohibited and seized.

**Why this is our best hook yet:** Deni's own terms name *pre-mix spirits* as an allowed category. Our pouches qualify, they're glass-free, and twelve of them sit comfortably inside a thirty-can allowance. The pitch writes itself — **"You get 30. Make 12 of them count."** Same allowance as everyone else, considerably better camp. All copy sells the camp bar, never the arena.
