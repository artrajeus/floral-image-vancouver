# Big Red Bash 2027 — candidate assessment

**Researched 7 Oct 2026.** Verdict: **yes, the strongest fit we have assessed** — but the campaign is a **November 2026** job, not a July 2027 one.

## The facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | **Tue 6 – Thu 8 July 2027** (weekdays verified) |
| Where | Big Red sand dune, ~35km west of Birdsville, far south-west QLD |
| Status | **The final Big Red Bash.** Confirmed by the organisers and on the official site |
| Tickets on sale | **Thu 12 November 2026, 10am AEDT** — 36 days from today |
| Payment | Pay in full or deposit, instalments from March 2027. Full refund if the event cannot go ahead |
| Capacity | Not published for 2027; past years ran 8,000–12,000, and the site says capacity is limited with high expected demand |
| Line-up | Missy Higgins, Hoodoo Gurus, Jessica Mauboy, Leo Sayer, The Living End, Birds of Tokyo, Shannon Noll |

**Why it is ending.** Founder Greg Donovan: the decision was made "with our head" after 13 years, citing logistical and operational challenges rather than cost alone — *"we'd rather go out on a high than risk the event being prevented from continuing by circumstances beyond our control."* The 2026 event was cancelled, 2025 was scaled back, 2023 and 2024 were hit by rain.

## Why the hook is the best of any event we have run

The mechanic we have been using — *the event's own rule is the scroll-stopper* — lands harder here than at any of the October three:

- **Fully BYO, and no alcohol is sold on site at all.** Same absolute scarcity as Dragon Dreaming. There is no bar to fall back on.
- **Glass is banned outright,** in a line that is already good copy: *"the only glass at Bashville should be condiment jars in your camp kitchen."*
- **BYO is allowed in the concert area.** This is the differentiator. Deni allows nothing into the Festival Arena; Savannah's precinct is completely dry. At Big Red you can carry your drink to the stage — so hands-free on a lanyard, glass-free, resealable is relevant across the *whole* festival, not just back at camp.
- **No published quantity limit** — just "eskies of a reasonable size" and drink responsibly. No allowance maths to work inside, unlike Deni's thirty cans.
- **Nearest resupply is the Birdsville Pub.** That is the only option, and it is a long way from the dune.

## The delivery problem solves itself

Birdsville is roughly 2,000km from Mitchell. We cannot deliver to the event and realistically cannot deliver to Birdsville on any sane timeline.

**That does not matter here, and it is worth understanding why.** Nobody receives mail at Big Red Bash — people drive for days to get there. The order goes to their **home address weeks out** and travels in the vehicle. So our transit constraint disappears, which is the exact inverse of the Savannah problem where FNQ transit was the binding constraint on the whole campaign.

The consequence for targeting: **the audience is not geographic.** It is "people holding a Big Red Bash ticket", spread nationally. Meta targeting has to be interest and behaviour based rather than the radius approach used for the October three.

## Timing — the actionable part

The instinct is to campaign in June 2027. That is wrong. The high-intent moment is **ticket on-sale, 12 November 2026**, when people are buying, planning the trip and thinking about what to pack. Capacity-limited with a deposit option means it will likely move fast.

Three beats:

1. **Nov 2026 — on-sale week.** Be in front of them while they are planning the trip. This is the one that matters.
2. **March 2027 — instalments fall due.** They are re-engaged with the trip and spending on it anyway.
3. **May–June 2027 — the pack-the-car run.** Order-by messaging, which is where the Canberra dispatch note belongs.

## Existing evidence this audience converts

We are not guessing. Our own Okendo reviews already name the sister event:

> *"Bought for the Mundi Mundi Bash, these were just the little pick me up... No glass makes them a perfect festival and travel addition"* — **Linda R., verified buyer**, on Espresso Martini (left the same review on Amaretto Whisky Sour and the 11-cocktail box)

And Mundi Mundi creative is the benchmark in `event-ad-creative-deck.md` — 5.5× ROAS at 11.7% CTR, against email converting one order per 1,199 recipients.

## The sister event, and the better long game

**Mundi Mundi Bash** (Mundi Mundi Plains, Silverton / Broken Hill, mid-August) is the same organiser's other festival and **is not ending**. Big Red is a one-off final-ever spike; Mundi is the repeatable annual.

Doing both is the obvious play — Big Red for the final-ever story, Mundi as the recurring fixture.

**Do not quote 2027 Mundi Mundi dates yet.** They are not confirmed, and the Broken Hill council listing contradicts itself (it gives 16 August 2027 as a Sunday; it is a Monday), which reads like last year's text carried over. The 2026 event ran 17–22 August at Silverton. Wait for the official announcement.

## Risks to weigh

- **Cancellation risk is real and recent.** 2026 was cancelled outright; the site had around six feet of floodwater on it, with the Bureau's evaporation modelling and the organisers expecting it dry by late February 2027. Tickets carry a full refund if it cannot go ahead — but we would be carrying stock. Do not over-commit inventory on this one.
- **Nine months of lead time** means a November campaign has to sell a product for a trip three quarters of a year away. The deposit structure helps: people are already paying in instalments for it.
