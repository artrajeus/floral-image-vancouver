## What this morning's restock email actually did

"Restock — Aussie Berry + Amaretto (+ Granny Smith seasonal)", sent 11:00am AEST Mon 10 Aug to 3 audiences (Adelaide Beer & BBQ list + Back in Stock — Notify Me list + Engaged 90 days segment).

| Metric | Result | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Recipients / delivered | 1,199 / 1,192 (99.4%) | Deliverability healthy |

| Click rate | **0.59%** (7 clicks) | The problem |
| Click-to-open | **2.5%** | Should be 10–15%. People open, then leave |
| Conversions | **1 order, $144** | $0.12 revenue per recipient |
| Unsubscribes | **10** | **More people unsubscribed than clicked** |

### The one number that matters

**Unsubscribes (10) exceeded clicks (7).** That's not a bad send, it's a warning: we are mailing this list more often than we're giving it a reason to click. The July Back in Stock send shows the same shape (1,978 recipients → 6 clicks, 12 unsubs, 1 order). The Adelaide Fest 3-email series: 38.6% → 27.7% → 22.1% open decay, 4 total clicks, **zero** orders across all three.

Consistent conclusion across every campaign in the last 30 days: **our email opens are fine and our email clicks are broken.** Product-news and restock emails are not a revenue channel for us right now.

## Meanwhile, where the money is actually coming from

Last 48 hours in Shopify: **27 orders / $3,931 — of which social (Meta) drove 22 orders / $3,251.** The Mundi Meta campaign is running 5.5x ROAS at 11.7% CTR.

Email produced 1 order today. Meta is producing ~11 orders a day. **Paid social is the Bash lever; email is a support act.** Any plan that relies on email to clear stock by Wednesday is betting on the weakest channel we have.

- Bash Pack inventory is now **26 units** (restocked from 16), $159 each ≈ **$4,134 of stock** to move.

## The plan

### 1. Meta is the lever — shift the budget there (do today)
Push the Mundi campaign hard for its final 48 hours with "Last post Wednesday" creative. At the campaign's observed rate ($400 → 18 orders), roughly **$300–400 over two days is what clears the remaining 26 units** — and unlike email, it reaches Bash attendees who aren't on our list. Hard stop end of day Wed 12 Aug.

### 2. Email — ONE send, not three
Today's restock already hit the engaged segment. Sending Bash emails Tuesday *and* Wednesday on top of it = 3 emails in 3 days to a list where unsubs already outrun clicks.

- **Share E1 "Forward to Your Camp Crew" — rescheduled to Tue 11 Aug 9:00am AEST** (`01KZKC3NEKR0B5AZC2ZGYDYZJ7`). It missed its Monday 10am slot (left in draft), which was lucky — it would have landed an hour before the restock email. This is a genuinely different message (deadline + BASHMATES discount + forward ask), so one send is defensible.
- **Share E2 "Last Post Today" (Wed) — hold it.** Only send if Tuesday's clicks beat 1.5% AND stock is still above ~15 units. Otherwise it's a third email in three days for marginal return.
- **Buyers E1 (comp launch) — drop it.** Its content is now covered by the share email. Keep Buyers E2 (18 Aug pre-departure) — that one goes to 5 people and earns its place.

### 3. Fix the real problem — the 2.5% click-to-open
This is the strategic finding, bigger than the Bash. Opens are healthy, so the list isn't dead and the subject lines work — people open, find no compelling reason to act, and leave. Three things to test on the next sends:
- **One offer, one button.** The restock email presented several products; the Mundi ad presented one thing to do. The ad converts at 5.5x.
- **A deadline in every send.** The only email content that has ever converted for us is event-deadline content.
- **Segment by intent, not by list.** Build a Klaviyo segment of "opened or clicked in last 30 days" and mail that group more; give the rest a break. Re-mailing non-openers is what's generating unsubscribes.

### 4. Then Gympie, properly
The Muster Pack is still **DRAFT with 0 inventory** and Gympie is 27–30 Aug (last post ~19 Aug). Once the Bash ships Wednesday, that's the next thing: allocate stock, activate the product, and run the Meta playbook — because Meta, not email, is what the Mundi data says works.

## Benchmarks to judge Tuesday's send against

Click rate >1.5% (vs 0.59% today) · unsubs below clicks · at least 3 orders. If it misses those, the answer isn't a better email — it's more Meta budget.
