# MXTology Festival & Event Travel Marketing Plan

**Prepared:** 8 August 2026
**Trigger:** Success of "The Bash Pack — 12 Glass-Free Cocktails for Mundi Mundi" (launched 3 Aug 2026)
**Goal:** Turn the Mundi Mundi play into a repeatable, always-on annual event marketing engine targeting people *travelling to* festivals and destination events across Australia.

| Inventory | Bash Pack: 16 units left with the shipping cutoff ~1 week away — restock/close-out decision needed now |

**Why the formula works (the actual mechanism):**

1. **Glass bans + BYO = pouches win by rule, not preference.** Nearly every camping festival and outback event bans glass. Beyond the Valley's own T&Cs literally spell out the allowance as "non-glass" containers. We are the only format purpose-built for the rule.
2. **Travel creates a deadline that isn't the event date.** Buyers must receive the box *before they leave home* — often 1–2 weeks before an outback event. Real urgency ("last post date"), no fake countdown timers.
3. **Ticket-holders are a pre-qualified, pre-committed audience.** Mundi Mundi 2026 sold out a month early. We don't need to sell the event — the organiser did. We only sell the packing list.
4. **Remote = no alternative.** Broken Hill/Birdsville have limited bottle shops, long queues, no cocktail options at camp. "Sorted before you leave" beats "figure it out out there."
5. **A named pack ("The Bash Pack — for Mundi Mundi") converts better than generic product.** Naming the event in the product title and ad is the whole trick — it reads as made-for-them.

## 2. Two demographics, two playbooks

### Stream A — "Nomads & Musters" (the Mundi Mundi crowd)
- **Who:** 45–70, caravanners/grey nomads + regional families. Plan trips weeks–months ahead. Facebook-native.
- **Events:** Mundi Mundi Bash, Big Red Bash, Gympie Muster, Birdsville Races, Deni Ute Muster, Tamworth, Parkes Elvis.
- **Angle:** "Twelve real cocktails, zero glass, one esky shelf." Civilised sundowner at camp. Pack-once convenience, free Express Post, quality-not-quantity.
- **Creative:** red dirt, camp chairs, esky shelf flat-lay, sunset over the van. Testimonial-style copy. Longer ad copy performs with this cohort.
- **Meta targeting:** event & organiser interest pages, lineup artists (e.g. The Teskey Brothers, John Butler for Mundi Mundi 2026), caravan/4WD/Big Lap interests, age 45+, geo = feeder states for the event.

### Stream B — "Doof & Fields" (the Strawberry Fields crowd)
- **Who:** 18–30, EDM/bush-doof festivalgoers. Instagram/TikTok-native. Walk-in camping, strict gate checks, BYO *limits*.
- **Events:** Strawberry Fields, Meredith, Golden Plains, Esoteric, Rabbits Eat Lettuce, Earth Frequency, Beyond the Valley, NYE on the Hill.
- **Angle:** "Passes the gate check." No glass, no cans to crush, packs flat, bar-quality at camp. Crew packs + lanyards (existing lanyard SKUs from hens range slot straight in).
- **Creative:** UGC-style vertical video, gate-check skit, tent flat-lay, "what my crew brought vs what I brought." Short, meme-literate copy.
- **Meta targeting:** festival + artist pages, 18–30, retarget IG engagers; creators/seeding to festival meme pages matter more than for Stream A.
- **Compliance note:** each event caps BYO volume and some limit *spirit-based* drinks — verify the pouch's classification per event's T&Cs before we claim "compliant" in an ad (BTV confirmed non-glass allowance; Strawberry Fields/Meredith/Deni limits to be confirmed each year).

## 4. The repeatable per-event playbook (SOP)

For each ★★★ event, clone this checklist with dates worked back from the **last-post date** (event departure date minus Express Post transit; for outback events assume travellers leave up to a week early):

- **T-8 weeks — Build.** Duplicate Bash Pack product → rename "The [Event] Pack", event-specific hero image, 12-pouch default (crew size variant for Stream B: 6-pack "tent pack" / 24-pack "camp pack"). Set Klaviyo segment + UTM convention `[event]-[year]`.
- **T-6 weeks — Launch.** Meta: 1 prospecting campaign (event/artist/lifestyle interests, feeder-state geo) + Advantage+ with event-named creative. Email E1 (story/"honest one" format) to full engaged list.
- **T-4 weeks — Mid-flight.** Email E2 (product detail / "what's actually in it"). Retargeting on. Kill losing creatives, feed spend to the winner. UGC/creator seeding for Stream B events.
- **T-2 weeks — Urgency.** Email E3 ("stock the esky/fridge"). Ad copy switches to "Last post for [Event]: [date]". This is the real conversion window — budget peaks here.
- **Last-post date — Hard stop.** Pause all event ads same day (spend after cutoff is wasted). Switch remaining traffic to next event on the calendar.
- **Event weekend — Presence.** Organic + community posts ("spot the pouch at [Event]"), capture UGC, run a tag-us competition.
- **T+1 week — Harvest.** Post-event email → Club subscription offer ("the fridge version of your festival box"). Tag buyers `[event]-2026` for next year's re-arm. Log results in the tracker (see §6).

**Golden rule:** we never market the event tickets — we market the packing list. Sold-out events are our *best* targets.

## 5. Channel notes

- **Meta (primary).** Structure: one always-on evergreen ASC + one event campaign at a time (two max if windows overlap, e.g. BTV + NYE on the Hill share creative). Budget guide: start $30–50/day per event campaign, scale in the T-2-week window if MER holds; hard stop at last-post. Age-gate 18+ (LDA), and use alcohol-compliant creative (no consumption-excess cues).
- **Klaviyo.** The Adelaide Fest 3-email arc is the template. Add two flows: (a) "Event pack browser abandonment" with last-post-date dynamic text; (b) annual re-arm — when an event's next-year dates are announced, auto-email last year's `[event]` tag ("Dates just dropped. Your box can be waiting.").
- **Organic/UGC.** Stream B lives on IG Reels/TikTok. One gate-check skit per Stream B event minimum. Repost every wild-camp pouch photo.
- **Partnerships (later).** Organiser bundles ("official cocktail of…") are expensive; start with caravan/festival Facebook groups, packing-list blogs and creator seeding instead.

## 6. Measurement

Track per event in one sheet: spend, orders, revenue, MER, AOV, new-customer %, list growth from event UTMs, post-event Club conversions. Success benchmarks from Mundi Mundi to beat: pack AOV ≥ $150, MER ≥ 3, ≥ 30% of pack buyers new to file.

## 7. Compliance checklist (per event, every time)

1. Confirm the event's current BYO policy and whether pouches fall under spirit-volume caps — never claim "gate-approved" without checking that year's T&Cs.
2. Liquor delivery rules by destination state (and dry-community postcodes around Broken Hill/Birdsville) before promising delivery windows.
3. Meta alcohol ad policy: 18+ targeting, no under-25 lookalike expansion where restricted, responsible-consumption framing.
