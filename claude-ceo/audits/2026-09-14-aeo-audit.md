## What is actually holding visibility back

**1. No measurement at all (score 0/10, and it gates everything else).**
Without Search Console there is no way to know which queries the store already
gets impressions for — which is the only reliable way to pick what to fix first.
Every keyword recommendation without it is a guess.

**2. Product schema could not be confirmed on the rendered page (3/15).**
The Tommy's Margarita page renders a good H1 and full body copy, but no JSON-LD
Product schema was visible in the fetched render. If it is genuinely absent, the
store is invisible to rich results and to the structured feeds AI answer engines
prefer. **This needs confirming in a browser before acting** — API and text-render
fetches both miss theme-injected JSON-LD, and flagging it as missing on that basis
alone would be exactly the mistake this audit's own guardrails warn against.

**3. No answer content (2/10).** A blog sitemap exists but there is nothing
answering the questions people actually ask before buying an RTD cocktail:
*are cocktail pouches any good · how many standard drinks in a cocktail pouch ·
what can you drink where glass is banned · best RTD cocktails Australia ·
MXTology vs Quips vs Curatif*. That last one matters — **Quips and Curatif are
named by name in three separate retailer replies this month.** If a customer or a
bottle-shop buyer asks an AI engine to compare them, there is nothing of ours for
it to cite.

**4. No off-site AI presence (0/10).** No third-party listicle, review-site or
Reddit footprint found. This is normally the largest single AI-visibility gap and
it is the hardest to fake — it has to be earned.

**5. `robots.txt` blocks AhrefsBot and SemrushBot.** Deliberate and defensible,
but it also blocks the store's own future competitive-research and
visibility-monitoring tools. Worth knowing before paying for one.

## Now / Later / Ongoing

### Now — this week, high gain, low effort
1. **Verify the store in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and
   submit `/sitemap.xml` to both.** Bing indexing is the prerequisite for ChatGPT
   citation. Roughly 15 minutes and it unlocks every other decision here.
2. **Confirm Product JSON-LD on a rendered product page.** If absent, that is a
   theme-level fix and belongs in Claude Code, not here.
3. **Fix the untitled product line.** The all-time sales report has a product with
   **no title carrying $11,691.61 across 165 orders** — the second-highest revenue
   line in the store. Either a deleted product or a data fault; either way the
   reporting is wrong until it is named.

### Later — bigger projects
4. **Five answer-first articles**, each with FAQPage or Article schema, on the
   questions above. The comparison piece against Quips and Curatif is the one with
   a buyer already asking for it.
5. **FAQ schema on the product pages** — standard drinks, shelf life, chilling,
   glass-free venues, shipping.
6. **Off-site presence** — get MXTology into Australian RTD round-ups and review
   sites. The Gordon Ramsay *Food Stars* angle is a real hook and it is currently
   only used on the store's own pages, where it cannot earn a citation.

### Ongoing
7. Publish answer content on a monthly cadence, hand the writing to the
   `blog-writer` skill so each post ships SEO + AEO ready with its schema.
8. Re-run this audit and re-score monthly.
