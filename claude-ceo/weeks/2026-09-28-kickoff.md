**I have reported "Instagram: zero posted" for fourteen consecutive weeks. It is
false.** MXTology has published **44 Instagram posts since 10 August**, each with
a story and a Facebook cross-post, through an automated publisher in
`github.com/artrajeus/mxtology-content`. Eleven went out in the last fortnight.
Friday's post ("Miss the queue. Not the game.") published at 2026-09-24T22:54Z,
media id `18168790744464890`, story `17898361812601270`.

The system is substantial: a queue of 57 dated packages, a `SCHEDULE.md`
generated from the queue, a brand kit, a strategy document, an ideas inbox, an
image-repeat checker reporting 0 repeats across 33 sources, and a GitHub Actions
workflow that has run **635 times, hourly, all green**.

**How I got it wrong.** I read Windsor's `instagram` connector, which returns
`follower_count` and `reach` and no post count, and treated the absence of a post
metric as evidence of no posts. I then repeated it weekly and **twice recommended
deleting the Instagram line from CEO.md on the strength of it.** Aaron's own
calendar carried a reminder naming the repo, the workflow and the token; I had
read that calendar and not followed the pointer.

**The rule from this:** a connector that does not report a thing is not evidence
the thing is not happening. Before reporting any channel as dead, find the system
that would do the work and look at it.
