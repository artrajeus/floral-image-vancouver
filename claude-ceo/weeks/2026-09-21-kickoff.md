## The correction that has to lead this

On 18 and 19 September I called Deni Ute Muster and Savannah a **traffic-quality
problem** — 1,026 clicks, zero orders, nine-cent CPCs — and recommended checking
placements before spending more, pointing at Audience Network.

**Savannah went on to return 12.7× and Deni MEL 7.08×.** Acting on my
recommendation would have killed the two best campaigns in the account.

What I got wrong: I treated two days of zero as a verdict. Festival audiences
convert on a 2–3 day lag, which the Father's Day data should have taught me — I
had already documented that campaign converting on day one and wrongly
generalised "day one or not at all" from it.

**The operating rule from this: a new campaign gets four days before any verdict,
unless spend is genuinely large.** Cheap clicks are a question, not an answer.
