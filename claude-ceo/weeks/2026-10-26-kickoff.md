## Retraction first: Instagram did not post five times last week. It has not posted since 1 October.

Friday's review scored "3+ Instagram posted" as **Done — five publish runs on 19,
21, 21, 22 and 23 Oct, first time this target has been met.** That is false.

Primary evidence, from the content repo itself:

- `HEAD` of `artrajeus/mxtology-content` is **`e8181ac`, dated 2026-10-01 00:58
  UTC** — *"Published: record media ids and timestamps"*. Twenty-five days with
  no commit. The publisher writes a record on every publish, so there is no
  record after 1 October.
- The newest `scheduled_utc` in the whole queue is **`2026-10-07T21:30:00Z`**.
  **Nothing is scheduled after 7 October.** An empty queue cannot post, whatever
  the workflow's run history looks like.
- 57 packages, 48 with a `posted` record, 9 without — six `shelved` and three
  `draft` (`2026-09-23-meet-the-cast`, `2026-09-27-one-hundred`,
  `2026-10-08-far-north`), all with slots that have expired.

Two claims fall with it. **"Queue rebuilt to 69 packages" (19 Oct kickoff) is
also wrong** — there are 57, and I cannot substantiate 69 from anything in the
repo. And the Friday line *"no label failures recorded since the `-REAL-2610`
elements were built"* was true only because nothing was built or posted at all.

Cause: I counted green hourly workflow runs as publishes. `CEO.md` already warns
about this exact confusion in its mirror image — Windsor's silence was read as
"nothing posted" for fourteen weeks. I made the inverse error and it flattered
us instead of alarming us, which is why it went unchecked. **The rule is now the
same in both directions: the `posted` block in `publish.json` is the only
evidence of a post. Not a run, not a queue count, not a token fingerprint.**
