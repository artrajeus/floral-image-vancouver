Spend fell 80%, revenue fell 81%. **There is no organic DTC base underneath the
advertising.** When a prospecting or occasion campaign runs, this store sells;
when only retargeting runs, it does roughly one order a day and the ads lose
money. Retargeting alone this week: $166.44 for a single $120 purchase.

That is the finding of the week and it is now well evidenced across three weeks
of data. The decision it forces is unchanged: repoint the Father's Day campaign
to the Bar Tab at $85–100/day, or pause the account entirely and stop paying
$35/day for nothing. Either is defensible. Continuing as-is is not.
