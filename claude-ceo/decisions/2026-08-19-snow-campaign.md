# Decision: kill the Snow Season campaign for 2026

**Date:** 2026-08-19 · **Status:** recommended, awaiting founder sign-off
**Trigger:** founder asked why `Snow Season 2026 — Espresso Martini — Seed` was
built on a traffic objective, and whether the money is better spent elsewhere.

## What the campaign actually is

`Snow Season 2026 — Espresso Martini — Seed`, campaign `120257653800600197`.
Objective **`LINK_CLICKS`** (traffic), campaign status **PAUSED**, ad set
`AU 18-54 — Broad (snow creative)` (`120257653806040197`) ACTIVE. Ran ~2.5h on
17 Aug: **$1.02, 111 impressions, 7 clicks, CPM $9.19**. Every other campaign in
the account is `OUTCOME_SALES`. Origin unconfirmed — not built by this agent.

**Meta fixes objective at creation.** Windsor's `update_campaign` states it
outright: *"objective is fixed at creation and cannot be updated."* Switching to
Sales means a new campaign, not an edit.

## Snow conditions — evidence as at 2026-08-19

| Measure | Value | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Spencers Creek depth (14 Aug) | **94.5 cm** | vs ~120 cm average — 21% below |
| Perisher upper / lower | **98 cm / 56 cm** | 38 of 47 lifts open |
| Last real snowfall | **10 August** | 13 cm; nine days ago |
| Next forecast snow | 1.6 cm Mon 24, 8 cm Tue 25 Aug | small, rain mid-week first |
| Thredbo Top Station max this week | **6 °C** | August average max is **0.6 °C** |
| Canberra | 17–18 °C Wed–Sun | vs 14.4 °C average |

Weatherzone: *"warm weather this week would likely erode the snowpack"*, and
**no significant snowfall until the end of next week at the earliest**. If late
August delivers nothing, 2026 becomes **only the third season since 1954 with a
peak below one metre** — the others are 1982 and 2006, both El Niño years.
Driver: El Niño plus a positive IOD — warmer, drier, weaker cold fronts.

**Founder's read was right on substance, with one correction:** the alps are not
being *rained* out. The rain this week is coastal; inland avoids significant
precipitation. The damage is **warmth**, not rain. And no resort has announced an
early close — Thredbo and Perisher run to **5 Oct**, Buller and Falls Creek to
**4 Oct**. The season is poor, not cancelled.

## What real skiers are saying (ski.com.au forums)

- Forum forecasters warned in May against *"booking anything from mid-August on"*.
- July on-the-ground reports: *"15 cm thick…over a lot of areas"*, *"High noon is
  mega icy today, huge sections of just ice"*, mountain-bike ruts still not
  covered.
- Sentiment trajectory across the thread: early optimism → cautious pessimism.
- Snowmaking covers only ~10–20% of terrain at Thredbo/Perisher, ~12% at Hotham,
  so there is no manufactured rescue for the other 80%.

## Decision

**Kill snow for 2026. Do not rebuild it as a Sales campaign.**

1. **The hook contradicts what the audience can see.** Snow creative sells deep
   powder to the exact people looking at 56 cm on the lower slopes and grass.
2. **The season's back half is the weak half**, and Australian peak depth
   normally lands late Aug / early Sept — this year it may not clear a metre.
3. **Small addressable market** even in a good year, against a mainstream RTD
   product.
4. **Clicks are not this account's constraint.** `MXT-TEST | Static Engine` is
   already `OUTCOME_SALES` at **14.09% CTR** — best in the account — for roughly
   one purchase on ~$225. Adding traffic to a conversion problem does nothing.

## Redeploy to Father's Day — Sunday 6 September 2026

18 days out. Mainstream, high-intent, gifting occasion; weather-independent;
much larger addressable audience; and the product is already gift-shaped (the
$132 "Whole Gang" 11-cocktail pack). **Creative already exists** — the
`2026-08-23 Father's Day — Dad's Bar Sorted (Teaser)` Klaviyo draft is sitting
unsent. Gift-shipping cutoff is realistically 1–2 Sept, so the ad build needs to
start this week, not next.

Keep the snow creative on file for 2027. The only scenario that revives it is a
genuine late-August dump, and the 24–25 Aug system forecast at 8 cm is not it.
